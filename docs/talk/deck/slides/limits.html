What this doesn’t show

Opponents

One seat against three identical, deterministic Experts, which may be poor at stopping a moon. A player could be exploiting that.

People

Nothing here has been tested against people, against a mix of opponents, or over whole games to 100.

1986 itself

The knowledge base is lost and its thresholds are reconstructed, so “matches” means matches the revival.

Effort

About 20 rule variants in one short round, and an LLM prompt that was not tuned. Both are for the future. Extracting more symbolic knowledge from the network is a promising path, not yet fully explored.
