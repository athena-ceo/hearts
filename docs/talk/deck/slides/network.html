  • Trained by self-play for 10 hours on one Mac, about 83 million deals, never against the Expert
  • Runs in pure Interlisp at 1.1 s a deal, and matched Python on 300 of 300 deals
  • Mostly moons: on an earlier 2,800-deal test (−3.09), its 191 moons to the Expert’s 69 gave −2.24 of the gain

Athena Decision Systems

24

An AlphaGo-style exercise, in miniature. It trained in Python, against itself and past snapshots, and then plays in Interlisp with no outside help. Look at its Thoughts windows: percentages, not reasons. Most of its gain comes from shooting the moon far more often than the Expert. One caution, which I’ll repeat: it played three identical, deterministic Experts, which may be poor at stopping a moon.
