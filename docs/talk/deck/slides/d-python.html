Why leave Medley at all? Three reasons. First, to modernize the game, extend it and publish it, so that people can play it without installing an emulator. Second, for a better experimental harness: a deal takes over a second in Medley, and benchmarks and rule experiments need many thousands of them. Third, and this is the one I care about most, to see whether some old expert system ideas can be brought out of the past and into the present, as rules people can read, change and govern. This isn’t a replacement. Medley stays the reference, and the next slide shows how the port is checked against it.
