The short answer is that my years on Xerox D-machines, at MIT, at Raytheon and at PARC, were the most exciting development period of my life. The HEARTS project folder has been sitting on my bookshelf for 40 years, and I’ve always wondered if there was some way to revive it. Then came the Medley port, which many of you made happen, and modern AI, and I thought there was a real chance. So I posted a question to this community: if I scan the source, what are the odds this could be loaded on the Medley emulator, and is KEE itself around somewhere? Many of you saw it. The answers turned out to be yes and no: the whole game runs on the current release, and we rebuilt the part of KEE the rules need, on LOOPS.
